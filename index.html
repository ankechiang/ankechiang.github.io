Meng-Fen Chiang is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Electronics and Electrical Engineering (ECE) at National Yang Ming Chiao Tung University (NYCU). Her research focuses on Trustworthy Multimodal Intelligence, spanning Large Language Models (LLMs), Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), and Knowledge-Grounded Reasoning Agents. Prior to NYCU, she was a Lecturer at The University of Auckland (UoA), a full-time Research Scientist at the Living Analytics Research Centre in Singapore, and Adjunct Professor at Singapore Management University (SMU). She received her Ph.D. from National Chiao-Tung University.
